PRESS-RETRACTION

Learning Press-Based Robotic Skin-Fold Retraction with Passive Compliance and Contact-Aware Action Semantics

Luhao Xie1, Zizheng Li1, Lifeng Zhu1,*, Jian Lu2, Bodong Fan1, Aiguo Song1

1Southeast University
2Zhongda Hospital, Southeast University

*Corresponding author

Paper Code

Abstract

Preparing an accessible skin surface is a prerequisite for reliable robotic tool entry. Automating this preparatory step is challenging because natural skin folds create nonplanar surfaces that move unpredictably during contact. This paper studies press-based robotic skin-fold retraction, which creates an operating region through normal pressing and tangential smoothing without grasping the tissue. We develop a passively compliant end effector that adapts to local surface variations, distributes contact loads, and preserves a central window for subsequent tool access. Contact-aware action semantics provide a task-level execution abstraction that maps a unified task-level action to free-space pose control or in-contact tangential smoothing and normal-force regulation under explicit safety constraints. The system is integrated with a real-world reinforcement learning pipeline using constrained demonstrations, online human intervention, and a multimodal reward classifier based on visual observations and wrist force-torque histories. Experiments on a physical skin-fold phantom show that the system succeeds in all 30 trials with lower peak normal force than the baselines, while component-wise ablations confirm the benefits of the main design choices.
